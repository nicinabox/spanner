---
title: Editing and retiring
---

Update a vehicle's details at any time, or retire a vehicle that is no longer being tracked. Retiring preserves all history.

![](/content/images/vehicle-edit-page.webp)

## In the app

The menu next to a vehicle's name on the [Vehicles page](/vehicles) contains **Edit**, **Import/Export**, **Share**, and **Retire**.

## Editing

**Edit** opens a form for the name, VIN, color, distance unit, and preferences. Changes take effect on save.

## Updating mileage

Mileage is not stored on the vehicle. It is carried by each record. To set or correct the current odometer reading without logging a service, use **Mileage Adjustment** from the vehicle's menu. The action creates a mileage-only record used by [Projections](/docs/history/mileage-and-projections).

## Retiring and restoring

Retiring hides a vehicle from the default list but keeps its records, reminders, and service schedules intact. Use it when you've sold the vehicle, finished tracking it, or want to set it aside without losing history. Retired vehicles remain accessible through the filter at the top of the Vehicles page.

Restoring brings a retired vehicle back. All attached data reappears unchanged. Pick **Retire** again on the vehicle (or un-retire from the Edit screen) to restore it.

## Deleting

To permanently remove a vehicle and its history, use the **Delete Vehicle** action in the **Danger Zone** at the bottom of the Edit screen. Deletion cannot be undone - all records, reminders, and service schedules for the vehicle are removed.
