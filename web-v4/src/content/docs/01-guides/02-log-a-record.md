---
title: Log a record
---



Goal: log a maintenance event for an existing vehicle.

1. Open the vehicle from the Vehicles page.
2. Go to the **History** tab.
3. Click the add action to open the record form.
4. Enter the **date** the service was performed.
5. Enter the **mileage** at the time of service.
6. Enter **notes** describing what was done.
7. Optionally add **cost**.
8. Add one or more **classifications** - for example, "oil change".
   - Spanner may suggest classifications based on your notes. Confirm or override.
9. Save the record.

## What you should see

The record appears in the vehicle's history list, ordered by date. If the record's classification matches an existing [Service Schedule](/docs/concepts#service-schedule), the schedule's next-due date and mileage reset to the new interval.
