---
title: Add your first vehicle
---



Goal: create a new vehicle and confirm it appears in the list.

1. Open the [Vehicles page](/vehicles) from the main navigation.
   - Existing vehicles (if any) and an **Add** action are shown.
2. Click the add action to open the new-vehicle form.
3. Enter a **name** for the vehicle.
4. Pick a **distance unit** - miles by default, or kilometers if that fits you better. Hours and nautical miles are also options for boats and equipment.
5. Optionally fill in the VIN and color. The preferences further down the form can be left at their defaults.
6. Save the vehicle.

## What you should see

The new vehicle appears at the top of the Vehicles list. Clicking it opens the vehicle's page with empty History, Tasks, and Notes tabs.
